### MLK I have a Dream

Hook/Attention Grabber
- “in what will go down in history as the greatest demonstration for freedom in the history of our nation”
- momentous days in human history
- context: standing in front of Lincoln memorial

Allusion
- Lincoln - “five score years ago”
- dec. of independence - “we hold these..”
- biblical language, prophetic language  → spiritual urgency
- “my country tis of thee” - ??some song??

bond/rapport
- “we”
- “together”
- “all God’s children”
- “brotherhood”

destination/purpose
- Lincoln’s promise is broken

figurative language / similes, metaphors
- mountaintop - truth, justice, also biblical
- stone of hope
- sweltering heat of oppression
- mountains of New York

parallelism
- to pray, to struggle, to go to jail, to stand up - repeating together

- “Let freedom ring”
