During a time of high racial tensions and protests in the US, African-American NFL football player Colin Kaepernick kneeled during the anthem. This led to him receiving backlash and not being signed again for the NFL.

In this advertisement published by Nike in 2018 the company is raising awareness to the issue at hand while also aiming to have a motivational effect of its consumers.

Seeing only and extreme close-up of Kaepernick’s face with a notable absence of editing the photograph, the direct eye contact engages the audience and creates a highly intimate atmosphere. The advertisement calls for the reader to believe.
