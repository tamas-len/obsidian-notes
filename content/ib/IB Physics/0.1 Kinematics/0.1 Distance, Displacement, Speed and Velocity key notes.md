### Tsokos reading p. 3-5.

> [!NOTE] The change in position is called displacement
> $$
  \Delta s = s_f - s_i
  $$

Distance --> scalar quantity
Displacement --> vector quantity

if v = const,
$$ s = s_i + vt $$
**In uniform motion**, the graph of
- *position* against time has a *non-zero gradient*
- *velocity* against time is a horizontal straight line (*zero gradient*)

The area under the v-t graph is displacement.
