>**Existential angst**:\
when you realize life has no meaning, you have to make your own choices, there are no excuses

>**Authenticity:**\
living in a way that’s true to who you are

>philosophers try to explain how to deal with the stress of trying to live authentically in a world that doesn’t care

> [!tip] Quick summary
> - **Dostoevsky:** You find authenticity through suffering + moral struggle.\
> - **Sartre:** You’re radically free → angst - face it→ choose authentically anyway.\
> - **Nietzsche:** World is meaningless → overcome nihilism → create your own values.\
> - **Camus:** Life is absurd → rebel by living fully.\
> - **De Beauvoir:** Authenticity = your freedom + others’ freedom.\
> - **Kafka:** Modern society = alienation → authenticity feels impossible.

### 3. Friedrich Nietzsche
==Overcoming Nihilism==

“God is dead” - old sources of meaning are gone, –> [[nihilism]]\
**“Nothing matters”**

but! instead of despair, create your own values. overcome nihilism.\
authenticity arises from embracing life even with suffering, chaos and absurdity

**Main counter-argument:**\
if everyone invents their own morality, that’s dangerous\
–> value system shouldn’t be based solely on individual will

### 4. Albert Camus
==Search for Meaning==

Camus says human life is **absurd** because:\
- we desperately want meaning,
- the universe doesn’t care.

But! instead of giving up, he says:
Authenticity =\
- acknowledging the absurd,
- but choosing to live *passionately* anyway.

His example: Sisyphus endlessly pushing his rock.\
“We must imagine Sisyphus happy.”

**Main counter-argument:**\
Isn’t just accepting the absurd kind of directionless? Can authenticity be achieved without deeper sense of purpose?

### 5. Simone de Beauvoir
==Authenticity includes other people’s freedom==

You can’t be authentic/free alone.\
- Society (especially sexism) limits people’s freedom.
- Real authenticity means also fighting for the freedom of others.
- You can’t be truly free if it is as someone else’s expense –> they must be free too.

Angst comes from:\
- struggling against restrictions,
- wanting self-realization,
- and feeling responsible toward others.

**Main counter-argument:**\
If you prioritize others too, is your authenticity yours or society’s?\
Balance btw personal authenticity and social responsibilities.\

### 6. Franz Kafka
==Alienation, Search for Meaning==

Feeling stuck, alienated - in a world that doesn’t make sense.\
His characters feel isolated and disconnected.\
Weird, absurd world - endless bureaucracy, nothing is fair or logical.

In _The Metamorphosis_, Gregor wakes up as a giant bug. He’s still trying to live a normal life, but the world treats him like he doesn’t exist. That’s Kafka: existential angst made literal.\

Kafka is kinda like saying “Why bother? Nothing I do seems to matter.”

focus on alienation feels hopeless - maybe his characters cannot ever achieve authenticity because the world is against them\
