AKA: slave morality
## notes

As opposed to the higher man, exists *The Herd*, the many

The herd is composed of
- The Last Man
- The Slave

#### The slave

weak human being, filled with “*ressentiment*”\
–> *ressentiment* is a sense of hostility towards an object identified to be the cause of one’s frustration\
–> Nietzsche says it is caused by *hatred of life generated by feelings of impotence in the face of an external reality felt to be overpowering and threatening*

ressentiment causes the slave to feel envy towards those not feeling it (the higher man) which motivates the slave to take vengeance\
the power comes from banding together, a communal feeling of power


“Herd Morality Inverts the Natural Values of Life”
–> herd morality deems successful as evil

> *”Finally - this is what is most terrible of all - the concept of the good man signifies that one sides with all that is weak, sick, failure, suffering of itself … the principle of selection is crossed - and ideal is fabricated from the contradiction against the proud and well-turned-out human being who says Yes, who is sure of the future, who guarantees the future - and he is now called evil. - And all this was believed, as morality!”* (Ecce Homo)

do NOT directly quote this, it is way too long - but is a great point to paraphrase and bring up –> as society and morality collapses, and the herd emerges, this new kind of morality forms

*Herd morality seduces those anxious and fearful in the face of uncertainty*

Herd Morality is not objective looking from the outside, the ideas of the herd rule in the herd, but must not reach beyond it
