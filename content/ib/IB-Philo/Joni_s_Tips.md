### notes

> To what extent is it possible that fundamental moral principles can be applied in every situation?

1. unpack the question, on a separate paper - let the idea linger (DO YOU HAVE TO LET IT LINGERRR)

- *To what extent* - not a yes or no, the exam is asking me to pass a balanced judgement
- *possible* - what is even possible?
- *fundamental moral principles* - are there fundamental principles? what are fundamental principles?
- ***every*** situation - words like *always, never, every* - always question them


#### paper 3

There’s part a and b , 10 and 15 points respectively. Part b takes longer time and is worth more points, so if you start with a, then you have to stop at one point writing it to have time for b. Or start with b, there are no rules saying in which order to answer questions.
