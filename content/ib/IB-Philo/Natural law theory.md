[[Aquinas]], the basic goods:

1. Life - self preservation, the drive to sustain life
	1. God  built all creatures with survival instinct
2. Reproduction
3. Educate one’s offspring
4. Seek God - whether we have been exposed to the idea of God or not
5. fifth one
6. sixth one
7. seventh one

- my life is valuable
- your life is like my life
- your life is valuable
- I shouldn’t kill you
- “do not kill” is a natural law

All basic goods leave to a prohibition and a positive injunction\
e.g.\
Basic good: life –> reproduction\
prohibition: don’t prevent reproduction (e.g. birth control, which the church opposes for this reason)\
positive injunction: promote life –> procreate

But then why do we all not follow natural law, if we were created to seek the good, and have the ability to recognize and seek it?\
Even plants and non human animals can do it\

**Aquinas**: Ignorance and emotion

*We see what we should do, but emotion overpowers our reason, and we fail to do the things we know we should.*

- Morality is grounded in God
- Reason to be moral: following the natural law makes our lives work better

### Critique

##### **What if I don’t believe God?**\
The whole premise fails.

##### **Is-ought problem:**
[[David Hume|Hume]]: *It’s fallacious to assume that just because something is a certain way, that means that it ought to be that way.*\
 –> this is what natural law theory does though, we see that creatures strive to survive, therefore have strong survival instincts - but is it ought to be that way?\
 –> striving for my survival could cause me to do things which seem immoral in the eyes of other people
