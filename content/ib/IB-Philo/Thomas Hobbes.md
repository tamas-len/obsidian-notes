### State of Nature

- no rules to govern our behaviour (no government, order, institution of any kind), life is *“solitary, poor, nasty, brutish and short”* (Hobbes)
- abundance of freedom –> but no security
- fear and agression dominates
- (so, due to fear, to achieve security you join the “[[Herd morality|herd]]” to survive, to be “strong”)?

Hobbes says three main areas of conflict in state of nature

> “*First, competition; second, diffidence; third, glory. The first makes men invade for gain, the second for safety, and the third for reputation.*”

From this the second one is interesting for us, because of safety.\
Constant state of anxiety, just from knowing that there *might* be a possibility of someone doing something.\
Hobbes says, to verify this, look at how you act: locking door, cars, avoiding areas of city etc. - taking steps to protect you and belongings EVEN in a society with a government - what is it like in the state of nature?
