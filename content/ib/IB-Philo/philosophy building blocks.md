### mind - body problem
what is the relationship between mind and body
can mental states be reduced to physical states - meaning: is mind a physical thing, or a different thing. does it exist physically? e.g. David Chalmers new dualism


1. René Descartes - substance dualism
	- mind and body are two different things
	- mind → thought. body → physical.
	- mind - body interact but different.
	- counter: but how do they interact? (interaction problem)

2. David Hume - empiricism, scepticism
	- no unified self → collections of perceptions - this is consciousness
	- mind: bundle of experiences → we cannot know the mind-body answer
	- counter: ?

3. Daniel Dennett - functionalism
	- mental states → defined by functional roles (not physical)
	- consciousness - series of processes
	- counter: ?

### free will vs determinism

1. Baruch Spinoza - hard determinism
	- everything is determined by nature, free will doesn’t exist
	- every event has a cause - domino
	- counter: how can we blame people for doing bad things? - undermines responsibility

2. Immanuel Kant - compatibilism
	- determinism + freedom can exist together
	- we are free if we act morally and based on reason, not impulse
	- natural law → but humans possess reason - not following desires
	- counter: my values are shaped by my environment - then did I choose? illusion

3. Jean-Paul Sartre - existentialism
	- “existence precedes essence” - you are “condemned to be free”
	- you create your own meaning
	- counter: if everyone invents their own, there is no objective moral
		- burden leads to despair, anguish
